Local SEO: A Step-by-Step Guide for Small Businesses

By Anil Sharma · Published 1 April 2023 · Updated 27 November 2026

Local SEO is the work that helps your business show up when people nearby search for what you sell. For many small businesses it's the most valuable marketing there is. Here is a step-by-step guide.

Step 1: Optimise your website for local search

  • Put your name, address and phone number on every page, usually in the footer
  • Create a dedicated page for each location
  • Use location keywords in titles, meta descriptions, headings and image alt text
  • Add LocalBusiness schema markup
  • Make sure the site is fast and mobile-friendly

Step 2: Claim and optimise your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile controls how you appear on Google Search and Maps.

  • Claim and verify the profile
  • Choose the most accurate primary category
  • Add services, products, hours, photos and a description
  • Post updates regularly
  • Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to all of them

Step 3: Build local citations

List your business on reputable directories (industry sites, local directories, Yelp, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect) with exactly the same details everywhere.

Step 4: Get links from local websites

  • Partner with complementary local businesses
  • Sponsor local events and get listed on their sites
  • Create local guides and resources others want to link to

Step 5: Measure and keep going

Track calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Business Profile, and watch local rankings across your area. Local SEO compounds when you keep adding reviews, photos and content.

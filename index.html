What I do

I help businesses get found, and get chosen

Since 2011 I've worked with online stores, clinics, builders, estate agents and local service businesses. I work directly in your ad accounts, analytics and website. I do what I say I'll do, when I said I'd do it, and I tell you plainly how it's going. There are no account managers and no hand-offs. You work with me.

Near you, wherever you are

An SEO Expert Near You, Wherever You Are

If you'd like someone close by, I'm in Himachal Pradesh, not far from Shimla, and happy to meet local businesses in person. If you're further away, we'll work over video calls. Either way, you're talking to the person actually doing the work.

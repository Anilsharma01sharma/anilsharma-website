Marketing Strategy

A Marketing Plan Built Around Your Goals

Before choosing channels, you need to know who you're selling to, why they should choose you, and what success looks like. I help you answer those questions with data, then turn the answers into a plan.

What’s Included

Target audience

Who your best customers are, what they search for and where they spend time.

Value proposition

Why customers should choose you, in words that work in ads and on your site.

Competitor research

What competitors rank for, advertise on and offer.

Channel mix

Which channels (SEO, Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Pinterest, email) to use first, and why.

Budgets & KPIs

Realistic budgets, targets and the numbers to watch each month.

Roadmap

A 90-day plan with clear owners and milestones.

How I Work

We start with a workshop and a look at your analytics, sales data and competitors.

You get a written strategy and a practical roadmap. I can carry it out with my team, or hand it to yours.

Strategy vs. Tactics

Tactics

Running ads or posting content without a plan. Busy, but hard to measure.

Strategy

Choosing the few actions that move your goals, and measuring them properly.

A good strategy makes every later tactic cheaper and easier to judge.

Questions

Usually two to three weeks, depending on how much data you have.

How I Work

I start by checking that the Pixel and Conversions API are recording the right events, without duplicates. Without that, Meta optimises for the wrong people.

Campaigns are kept simple, with a few well-funded ad sets rather than dozens of small ones, and are improved through regular creative tests.

Paid vs. Organic Facebook

Organic

Free, and good for community and trust, but reach is small and unpredictable.

Paid

Reaches exactly who you choose, at the scale you choose, with results you can measure.

The two work best together: organic builds credibility, and paid brings it to the people most likely to buy.

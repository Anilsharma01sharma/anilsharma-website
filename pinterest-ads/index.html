How I Work

Pinterest rewards planning. Campaigns for a season start weeks earlier than they would on other platforms, so your pins are already there when people start searching.

I set up tracking, then test audiences and creative before scaling the combinations that drive sales.

Pinterest vs. Other Paid Advertising

Pinterest Ads

Visual and discovery-led, reaching people early in their buying journey, often at lower cost per click.

Search & social ads

Broader formats and audiences, better for demand that already exists.

Pinterest suits brands with a strong visual product. It works best alongside Google and Meta, not instead of them.

How I Work

First I check tracking. Smart bidding is only as good as the conversions it is fed, so broken or double-counted conversions get fixed before anything else.

Then I build or restructure campaigns around your goals (leads, sales or calls), set realistic targets, and optimise weekly: search terms, bids, ads, audiences and landing pages.

Google Ads vs. Other Paid Channels

Google Ads

Reaches people at the moment they search. You pay per click, and intent is high.

Social ads

Meta, Pinterest and LinkedIn build demand among people who aren't searching yet, priced per impression or per click.

Search captures demand and social creates it. The right mix depends on your product and budget.

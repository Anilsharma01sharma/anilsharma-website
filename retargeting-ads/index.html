How I Work

I build audiences from real behaviour, such as product views, cart adds and form starts, and exclude people who have already converted.

Messages change with each stage: a reminder, then a reason to buy now, then social proof, all within sensible frequency limits.

SEO vs. Paid Retargeting

SEO

Brings new visitors to your site from search over time.

Retargeting

Turns more of those visitors into customers after they leave.

They work together: SEO and ads fill the funnel, and retargeting stops it leaking.

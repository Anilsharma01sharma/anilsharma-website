How I Work

Instagram ads run through Meta Ads Manager, so they are planned together with your Facebook campaigns to avoid two campaigns competing for the same people.

I test creative regularly, because on Instagram the creative does most of the targeting.

Instagram vs. Facebook Ads

Instagram

Visual, younger-skewing and strong for fashion, beauty, lifestyle and products that photograph well.

Facebook

A broader audience and more placements, often cheaper per result for lead generation.

Most accounts run both, and Meta's automatic placements decide where each impression performs best.

Questions

Do I need video?

Not always. Short vertical video helps in Reels and Stories, but strong images and carousels work too.

Can you promote existing posts?

Yes, although purpose-built ads usually perform better than boosted posts.

Is Instagram right for B2B?

Sometimes. For most B2B offers, LinkedIn or Google Search is a better first channel.

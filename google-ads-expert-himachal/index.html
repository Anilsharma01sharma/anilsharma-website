The process

How I Work

The first thing I check is your conversion tracking. Google's bidding learns from it, so if it's broken or counting things twice, the account is learning the wrong lessons. I fix that before touching anything else.

Then I build or tidy up the campaigns around what you actually want: calls, form leads or online sales. Every week I go through search terms, bids, ads and landing pages, and cut what's wasting money.

  2. 01LookI go through what you have and find what's holding you back.
  3. 02PlanWe agree what to do first and what it should achieve.
  4. 03DoI make the changes and launch the campaigns myself.
  5. 04Improve & reportI keep testing, and send you a short monthly update.

Google Ads vs. Other Paid Channels

Search catches people who already want what you sell; social ads find people who don't know they want it yet. The right mix depends on your product and budget, and I'll tell you which I'd start with.

Google Ads

Reaches people at the moment they search. You pay per click, and intent is high.

Social ads

Meta, Pinterest and LinkedIn build demand among people who aren't searching yet, priced per impression or per click.

Near you

Spending only where your customers are

If people need to visit or call you, there's no point paying for clicks from the other side of the country. I set your ads to the areas you actually serve, add your phone number and address, and cut the places that waste money.

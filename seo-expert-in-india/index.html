How I Work

Every project starts with an audit of your website, rankings and analytics. From that I agree a plan with you, with clear priorities and KPIs, before anything changes.

Then the work happens in small, visible steps. You get a change log you can read at any time and a monthly report in plain English: what moved, why, and what comes next.

SEO vs. Paid Advertising

SEO

Builds rankings you own. It takes a few months to compound, but the traffic keeps coming without paying for every click.

Paid ads

Buys visibility from day one and is ideal for testing offers, but stops the moment the budget does.

Most of my clients use both: ads for immediate leads, SEO to lower the cost of every lead over time.

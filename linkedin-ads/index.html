How I Work

LinkedIn clicks are expensive, so the offer matters more than anywhere else. We agree a clear, valuable offer, such as a guide, an audit or a demo, before spending.

I start with tightly defined audiences, then widen only when lead quality holds up.

LinkedIn Organic vs. Paid

Organic

Free reach through your profile and company page. Slow to build, but it creates a loyal following.

Paid

Reaches precise audiences immediately, with full tracking and predictable volume.

Organic builds credibility, and paid makes sure the right people actually see it.

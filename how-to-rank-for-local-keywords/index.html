How to Rank for Local Keywords: A Practical Local SEO Guide

By Anil Sharma · Published 1 April 2023 · Updated 27 November 2026

When people search for something nearby, like "plumber near me" or "dentist in Boise", Google shows a short list of local businesses. Here's how to get yours into that list.

1. Find the right local keywords

Combine your services with your locations: "plumber in Boise" or "dentist Idaho Falls". Google Keyword Planner and Moz Keyword Explorer show volumes, and Google's autocomplete shows how people phrase them.

2. Optimise your website for local search

  • Show your business name, address and phone number (NAP) consistently
  • Create a page for each location or service area you serve
  • Use local keywords in titles, headings and copy
  • Embed a map and make the site fast and mobile-friendly

3. Optimise your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is what appears in the map pack. Choose accurate categories, add services, photos and opening hours, post updates and reply to every review.

4. Build local citations

Citations are mentions of your name, address and phone number on other sites. List your business on relevant directories, industry sites and your local chamber of commerce, with identical details everywhere.

5. Earn local backlinks

  • Sponsor or take part in local events
  • Write for local blogs and news sites
  • Offer expert comment to local journalists
  • Partner with complementary local businesses

6. Add local schema markup

LocalBusiness schema tells search engines your business type, address, hours and contact details. Add review markup only where it follows Google's guidelines.

7. Track rankings across your area

Local rankings change street by street. A grid-based tracker such as Local Falcon shows where you rank across your whole service area, not just from one spot.

There's no trick to local SEO. It's accurate details everywhere, a steady trickle of reviews and pages that genuinely help local customers, kept up month after month. Get in touch.

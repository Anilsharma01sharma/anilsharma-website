Landing Pages

Landing Pages That Turn Clicks into Customers

Sending paid traffic to a generic homepage wastes budget. A focused landing page with one offer and one action can double the leads from the same ad spend.

How I Work

We agree one goal per page. Then I write the copy, design the layout and build it, usually within a couple of weeks.

After launch I watch the numbers and run tests, so the page improves rather than standing still.

Landing Page vs. Full Website

Landing page

One offer, one action and no distractions. Best for ad campaigns and promotions.

Full website

Many pages for many visitors. Best for SEO, credibility and browsing.

You need both: the website builds trust over time, and landing pages convert campaign traffic.
